| Criteria | Requirements |

## 03. Target Decision Makers

- Founder
- Co-Founder
- Chief Executive Officer
- Chief Operating Officer
- Chief Marketing Officer
- Managing Director
- Head of Growth
- Marketing Director
- Sales Director
- Business Development Manager

Select relevant roles based on client requirements.

## 04. Required Data Fields

- Company Name
- Company Website
- Industry
- Company Size
- Company Location
- Decision Maker Name
- Job Title
- Professional LinkedIn URL
- Business Email
- Email Verification Status
- Research Source
- Research Date

## 05. Lead Qualification

| Criteria | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Industry Relevance | 25% |
| Decision Maker Relevance | 25% |
| Geographic Match | 15% |
| Company Size Match | 15% |
| Data Completeness | 10% |
| Contact Verification | 10% |
| Total | 100% |

### Qualification Levels

- 80–100: High Priority
- 60–79: Medium Priority
- Below 60: Low Priority

This scoring model is illustrative and can be customized
according to campaign requirements.

## 06. Exclusion Criteria

- Companies outside the target industry
- Irrelevant job titles
- Duplicate records
- Inactive businesses
- Companies outside target locations
- Contacts that do not meet campaign requirements

## 07. Research Methodology

1. Review client requirements.
2. Define ideal customer profile.
3. Identify relevant companies.
4. Research decision makers.
5. Collect permitted business information.
6. Validate and standardize data.
7. Remove duplicate records.
8. Perform final quality assurance.
9. Prepare the lead database.

## 08. Quality Assurance Checklist

- [ ] Industry match confirmed
- [ ] Company size confirmed
- [ ] Target location confirmed
- [ ] Decision maker relevance checked
- [ ] Business contact validated
- [ ] Duplicate records removed
- [ ] Data formatting standardized
- [ ] Research sources documented
- [ ] Privacy requirements reviewed
- [ ] Final quality assessment completed

## 09. Data Privacy

Research must respect applicable privacy regulations
and platform terms of service.

Confidential client data and private personal information
must not be published in public repositories.
